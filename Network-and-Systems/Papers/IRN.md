**Paper:** [Revisiting Network Support for RDMA]([Revisiting network support for RDMA | Proceedings of the 2018 Conference of the ACM Special Interest Group on Data Communication](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3230543.3230557))

This paper discusses the application of PFC on RDMA fabric, and whether or not we *actually* need it, and what to do if we just want to let go of it.
The paper discusses the troubles that PFC brings, why we should worry about them, and proposes what seems to be a reasonable way to fix it by slightly changing the RDMA fabric configuration (this paper targets [[RoCE]]).
